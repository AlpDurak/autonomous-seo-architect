# Ecommerce SEO Playbook

Use when the site sells products, catalogs, subscriptions, or marketplace goods.

## Priority Checks
- Audit category, subcategory, product detail, variant, faceted navigation, search, cart, and out-of-stock templates.
- Validate indexation rules for filters, sorts, parameters, pagination, and internal search.
- Require crawlable product names, prices, availability, images, reviews only when visible, and shipping/return policy signals.
- Validate `Product`, `Offer`, `AggregateRating`, `Review`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`, and merchant-related structured data when applicable.
- Check image SEO: descriptive filenames, dimensions, alt text, product angles, CDN transformations, and image sitemap coverage.

## Common Opportunities
- Category intro copy that answers buying criteria without burying products.
- Programmatic long-tail category pages only when inventory and differentiation are real.
- Internal links from guides to commercial categories and products.
- Out-of-stock handling: preserve evergreen URLs, offer alternatives, avoid soft 404s.
