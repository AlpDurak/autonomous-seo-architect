# Local SEO Playbook

Use when the site targets local services, physical locations, service areas, or Google Business Profile visibility.

## Priority Checks
- Check NAP consistency: business name, address, phone, hours, service areas, and location pages.
- Validate `LocalBusiness`, `Organization`, `PostalAddress`, opening hours, geo, and sameAs fields when the data is present.
- Audit location/service landing pages for unique local proof, reviews, staff, photos, parking/access details, neighborhoods, and FAQs.
- Ensure local pages do not mass-generate thin doorway pages.
- Recommend Google Business Profile work only as external-account guidance unless the user authorizes access.

## Common Opportunities
- Service plus city pages with differentiated proof and local intent.
- Location hub pages linking to services and nearby areas.
- Review acquisition and response workflows as recommendations.
