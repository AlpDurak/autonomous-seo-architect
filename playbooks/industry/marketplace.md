# Marketplace SEO Playbook

Use when the site connects buyers and sellers, hosts listings, vendors, profiles, jobs, rentals, or user-generated inventory.

## Priority Checks
- Audit listing, category, location, profile, search, filter, pagination, and empty-state templates.
- Define indexation rules for combinations of category, geography, filters, availability, and search results.
- Detect duplicate/thin listings, expired inventory, seller-generated spam, and crawl traps.
- Validate `Product`, `Offer`, `LocalBusiness`, `JobPosting`, `ItemList`, `BreadcrumbList`, or domain-specific schema only when visible.
- Apply UGC link controls and trust moderation signals.

## Common Opportunities
- Programmatic category plus location pages with real inventory thresholds.
- Internal links from high-demand categories to high-supply geographies.
- Expired listing strategy: alternatives, redirects, archive/noindex rules.
