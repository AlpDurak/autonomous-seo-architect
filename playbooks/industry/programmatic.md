# Programmatic SEO Playbook

Use when the site generates many pages from templates, datasets, directories, filters, or combinations.

## Priority Checks
- Identify template families, data sources, parameter space, internal-linking rules, and indexability thresholds.
- Detect thin, duplicate, near-duplicate, empty, or low-demand generated pages.
- Require each indexable template to have unique value: data, inventory, comparison, local proof, examples, media, or user utility.
- Validate canonicals, noindex rules, sitemap inclusion, pagination, and crawl budget risks.
- Sample templates across best, median, and worst data quality.

## Common Opportunities
- Define quality gates before pages enter the sitemap.
- Add hub pages and drill-down paths that keep important generated pages within 2-3 clicks.
- Create monitoring for template regressions, soft 404s, and index bloat.
