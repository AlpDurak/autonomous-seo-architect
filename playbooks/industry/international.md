# International SEO Playbook

Use when the site targets multiple countries, languages, currencies, or regional subdirectories/domains.

## Priority Checks
- Validate locale routing, hreflang reciprocity, x-default, canonical/hreflang conflicts, language selectors, and translated metadata.
- Check that localized pages are not merely machine-translated shells without local relevance.
- Validate currency, units, shipping, legal, address, and contact localization where relevant.
- Check sitemap hreflang and per-locale sitemap coverage.

## Common Opportunities
- Consolidate duplicate language variants with correct canonicals and hreflang.
- Build locale-specific keyword clusters instead of direct translation of head terms.
- Add local trust signals, policies, and examples.
